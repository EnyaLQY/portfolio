      - "<strong>How might we introduce a fast-growing local marketplace app through a website that feels both simple and established?</strong>"
      - "iDeal is a second-hand marketplace app for buying and selling used goods within the State College community. In a two-person team, I led the visual and UX design of its official website while collaborating closely with the lead programmer on feasibility and implementation."
      - "The website needed to explain the product, reflect iDeal's approachable brand, support future community growth, and give stakeholders a polished public-facing presence."
